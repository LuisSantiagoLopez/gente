# Your privacy in Gente

## What we promise

- Your AI lives on your computer. Your chats never leave it.
- No usage limits. Message as much as you want.
- Nobody reads your data, trains on it, or sells it. Not us, not anyone.
- No ads. Ever.

When Gente works online for you (searching, browsing, apps), only what that task needs goes out.

## What stays on your computer

- Your chats, your bots' memory and your files.
- The AI model. It runs on your computer, not on our servers.
- Gente sends no analytics, no error reports and no tracing. These are switched off in the app
  itself, so a settings file can't turn them back on.

## What goes out, and when

These only happen during a task that needs them, or after you turn them on.

| What               | Who receives it                                       | What they get                                                  | When                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Web search         | DuckDuckGo, or the search service Gente uses          | The search words your bot writes                               | When your bot decides it needs to look something up                                                                                 |
| Reading a web page | That website                                          | The page address                                               | When your bot opens a page                                                                                                          |
| Cloud computer     | E2B                                                   | What your bot does on that computer: commands, files, browsing | When a task uses the cloud computer                                                                                                 |
| Cloud browser      | Browserbase                                           | The pages your bot visits and what it types there              | When a task uses the cloud browser                                                                                                  |
| Connected apps     | Composio, and the app you connect (for example Gmail) | What the task needs from that app                              | Only after you connect an app                                                                                                       |
| Coding docs        | Context7                                              | The library question your bot asks                             | Only after you add Context7                                                                                                         |
| Telegram           | Telegram                                              | Messages in that Telegram chat                                 | Only after you connect Telegram                                                                                                     |
| Payments           | Stripe Link                                           | The amount, the shop and a short reason                        | Only when you approve a purchase                                                                                                    |
| Voice typing       | Apple, on your Mac                                    | Your voice                                                     | Gente uses on-device voice typing. If your language has no on-device model, macOS may send the audio to Apple to turn it into text. |
| Updates            | GitHub                                                | Your app version and IP address                                | When Gente checks for an update                                                                                                     |
| First setup        | The model download sites                              | Your IP address                                                | Once, while Gente downloads its AI                                                                                                  |

Each of these companies follows its own privacy terms for what it receives.

## Link icons and web images

Gente does not load icons or images from websites in your chats unless you turn on
**Settings → Load web images automatically**. Loading them tells that website someone has its link.
