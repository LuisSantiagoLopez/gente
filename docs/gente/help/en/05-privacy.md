# Privacy

Gente is private like a conversation at home. Your chats never leave your computer. Nobody reads them, trains AI on them or sells them.

## What stays on your computer

- Your chats and everything your bots remember.
- The AI model. It runs on your computer, so your questions are not sent to an AI company.
- Your account. It is a local account on this computer.
- Keys and tokens. They are locked in your computer's keychain (Keychain on Mac, the Windows credential store on Windows).

## What leaves your computer, and when

Some things need the internet. Here is the full list:

- **Downloads:** Ollama, Gente's model and Gente's updates. These only download. Nothing of yours is sent.
- **Apps you connect:** when a bot uses Gmail, Calendar or another app you connected, it sends that app what the task needs, like an email you asked it to send.
- **Telegram, if you connect it:** messages you send your bot in Telegram travel through Telegram, like any Telegram message. Your chats in the Gente app don't.
- **Online work:** when a task runs on a cloud computer, that computer visits the site the task needs. Your chat history is not sent there.
- **Images and videos you make:** your description goes to our image and video service, fal.ai, to make the file. fal.ai keeps no record of the request, and its copy of the file is deleted after 1 hour. Gente saves the file on your computer; our temporary cloud copy is deleted after 30 days.
- **Support, only if you choose:** a message you send to a person, and an error report if you allow it.

## Error reports

When Gente hits a problem it can't fix by itself, it can make an error report. Gente asks you before sending one. You can choose **Always send**, **Ask each time** (the default) or **Never send** in Support settings.

An error report has:

- Gente's version, your system (like "macOS 26, 16 GB") and error codes.
- Technical error details and the last lines of Gente's service logs.

Before anything is sent, Gente removes chat text, names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, keys, tokens, links and your folder names. You can click **See what's in it** to read the exact report first.

If you choose **Always send**, Gente also sends anonymous counts (Gente opened, Gente crashed, setup finished). They help us make sure an update is safe before everyone gets it. They have no chat content and no personal data.

## Delete your data

Your data is in Gente's folder on your computer (see [where the logs are](troubleshooting#where-the-logs-are) for the location). Deleting that folder deletes everything. We have no copy.
