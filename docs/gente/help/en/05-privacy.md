# Privacy

Your AI is private like a conversation at home. Your chats never leave your computer. Nobody reads them, trains AI on them or sells them.

## What stays on your computer

- Your chats and everything your bots remember.
- The AI model. It runs on your computer, so your questions are not sent to an AI company.
- Your account. It is a local account on this computer.
- Keys and tokens. They are locked in your computer's keychain (Keychain on Mac, the Windows credential store on Windows).

## What leaves your computer, and when

Some things need the internet. Here is the full list:

- **Downloads:** Ollama, Your AI's model and Your AI's updates. These only download. Nothing of yours is sent.
- **Apps you connect:** when a bot uses Gmail, Calendar or another app you connected, it sends that app what the task needs, like an email you asked it to send.
- **Telegram, if you connect it:** messages you send your bot in Telegram travel through Telegram, like any Telegram message. Your chats in the Your AI app don't.
- **Online work:** when a task runs on a cloud computer, that computer visits the site the task needs. Your chat history is not sent there.
- **Images and videos you make:** your description goes to our image and video service, fal.ai, to make the file. Your AI saves it on your computer. A temporary cloud copy is deleted after 30 days. fal.ai has its own rules: by default it keeps the request (your description and the result) for 90 days. See [fal.ai's data retention](https://fal.ai/docs/model-apis/data-retention).
- **Support, only if you choose:** a message you send to a person, and an error report if you allow it.

## Error reports

When Your AI hits a problem it can't fix by itself, it can make an error report. Your AI asks you before sending one. You can choose **Always send**, **Ask each time** (the default) or **Never send** in Support settings.

An error report has:

- Your AI's version, your system (like "macOS 26, 16 GB") and error codes.
- Technical error details and the last lines of Your AI's service logs.

Before anything is sent, Your AI removes chat text, names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, keys, tokens, links and your folder names. You can click **See what's in it** to read the exact report first.

If you choose **Always send**, Your AI also sends anonymous counts (Your AI opened, Your AI crashed, setup finished). They help us make sure an update is safe before everyone gets it. They have no chat content and no personal data.

## Delete your data

Your data is in Your AI's folder on your computer (see [where the logs are](troubleshooting#where-the-logs-are) for the location). Deleting that folder deletes everything. We have no copy.
