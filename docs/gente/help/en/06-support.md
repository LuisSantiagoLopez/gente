# Get help

Open **Help → Your AI Support** in the app menu, or press **Cmd+Shift+/** on a Mac or **Ctrl+Shift+/** on Windows.

## Ask a question

Type your question in plain words. Your AI's own model answers from these help articles, on your computer. Your question is not sent anywhere.

Under each answer you see which article it came from. Click it to read the whole article.

## One-tap fixes

When something is broken, the answer can include a fix button:

- **Restart Your AI's services:** starts the model server again and restarts your workspace.
- **Download the model again:** removes only Your AI's model and downloads it fresh.
- **Clear the cache:** removes Your AI's temporary files. Chats, bots and settings stay.
- **Link Telegram again:** opens the Telegram window so you can paste a fresh token.

No fix deletes your chats, bots, memory or settings.

## The health dot

The dot at the top of Support shows how Your AI is doing:

- **Green:** all good.
- **Yellow:** something is starting or needs a small step from you.
- **Red:** something is broken. Click the dot to see what and fix it.

Your AI checks itself every few minutes and fixes most problems on its own, like restarting a stopped model server.

## Talk to a person

If the answers and fixes don't solve it, Support shows **Talk to a person**. Add your email and what happened. A person replies by email, usually within a day.

Tick **Include an error report** to send a report with it. It has no chats and no personal data.

## Help without the app

If Your AI won't open at all, use the support form on our website. Tell us your computer (Mac or Windows) and what you see.
