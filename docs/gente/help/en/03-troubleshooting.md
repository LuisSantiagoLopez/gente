# Fix a problem

Your AI checks itself every few minutes and fixes most problems quietly. If something still feels wrong, open **Help → Your AI Support**. The dot at the top shows Your AI's health. Click it to see each check and fix it with one tap.

## Your AI is slow

- The first answer after opening Your AI can take a few seconds while the model wakes up.
- Long tasks with many steps take longer than a quick question. You can keep chatting while they run.
- Close apps that use a lot of memory, like games or video editors. Your AI's model needs about 11 GB.
- If answers stay slow, tap **Restart Your AI's services** in Support.

## The model server stopped

Your AI uses Ollama to run its model. If Ollama stops, Your AI starts it again by itself within a minute. If it doesn't, tap **Restart Your AI's services** in Support. If that doesn't help, restart your computer.

## The model is missing or damaged

A download can break if the computer sleeps or the disk fills up in the middle. Your AI notices and tells you. Tap **Download the model again** in Support. It downloads 8.9 GB again, so use a good connection.

## Your disk is full

Your AI needs a few GB free to keep working, and about 11 GB to download the model.

1. Empty the Trash or Recycle Bin.
2. Delete big files you don't need, like old downloads or videos.
3. Tap **Clear the cache** in Support. This removes Your AI's temporary files only. Your chats stay.

## Another app is using Your AI's address

Your AI's workspace talks over a few local addresses (ports) on your computer. If another app takes one, Your AI picks a free one by itself. If the workspace still won't start, tap **Restart Your AI's services** in Support.

## Part of the app is missing

This happens when an update didn't finish or a file was removed. Your AI reports it, and an update repairs it. To fix it now, download Your AI again and install it over the old one. Your chats stay.

## The workspace won't start

Your AI tries again three times by itself. If it still can't start:

1. Tap **Restart Your AI's services** in Support.
2. Restart your computer.
3. If it still fails, tap **Talk to a person** in Support.

## Not enough memory

Your AI needs a computer with at least 16 GB of memory. If yours has 16 GB or more and you still see this, close other apps and press **Try again**.

## No internet during setup

The first setup needs the internet to download the model. If the connection drops, Your AI waits and picks up where it stopped. Press **Try again** when you're back online.

## Telegram stopped answering

1. In Telegram, open **@BotFather** and send `/mybots`. Check that your bot is still there.
2. In Your AI, open Support and tap **Link Telegram again**, or choose **Connect Telegram…** in the app menu.
3. Paste the token from BotFather and click **Connect**.

If you made a new token in BotFather, the old one stops working. Paste the new one.

## An update didn't install

- **Mac:** move Your AI into the Applications folder. Updates can't install while Your AI runs from a disk image or the Downloads folder.
- Free up some disk space.
- Quit Your AI and open it again. If it still fails, download the latest version and install it over the old one.

## Mac says Your AI can't be opened

Right-click Your AI in Applications, choose **Open**, then **Open** again. You only do this once.

## Where the logs are

You never need to read logs. If a person from support asks for them:

- **Mac:** your user Library, in Application Support/Gente/stack/logs
- **Windows:** your AppData/Roaming/Gente/stack/logs folder

Older installs named Burrow keep using the Burrow folder.

## Get help from a person

In Support, ask your question. If the answer and the fixes don't solve it, tap **Talk to a person**. Write what happened and your email. A person replies, usually within a day. You choose whether to include an error report.
