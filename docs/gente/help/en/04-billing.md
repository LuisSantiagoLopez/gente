# Plans and billing

Gente runs its AI on your computer, so you don't pay per question. You pay for the plan you choose.

## Plans

| Plan | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Free** | 10 messages to try Gente. |
| **Pro** | Chats with your bots on your computer, plus hours of online work each month. |
| **Max** | Everything in Pro, with more hours of online work each month. |

The current price of each plan is shown when you choose it.

## Hours of online work

Most of what Gente does runs on your computer. **Online work** is the part that runs on a computer in the cloud, like a full browser that books a ride for you. Your plan includes a number of hours of it each month.

Time counts only while a cloud computer is working on your task. Your chats stay on your computer either way.

## Change or cancel your plan

Open **Help → Gente Support** and ask. A person can change or cancel your plan for you. Your chats and bots stay on your computer whatever plan you have.

## Refunds and billing questions

For a refund, a double charge or anything about a payment, open Support and tap **Talk to a person**. Never send your full card number in a message. We never ask for it.

## Purchases a bot makes

Things a bot buys for you are not part of your plan. You approve each one yourself in Link, and Link charges the card you chose there. Gente never stores your card.
