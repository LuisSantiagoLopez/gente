# What Gente can do

Gente gives you AI helpers, called bots, that chat with you, use tools, follow routines and remember what matters to you. The thinking happens on your computer.

## Bots and chats

A bot is a helper with a name, a job and its own memory. Each bot has one ongoing chat with you, so it remembers what you talked about before.

- Press **Cmd+N** (Mac) or **Ctrl+N** (Windows) to start something new.
- You can keep chatting while a bot works. New messages never wait for old work to finish.

## Group chats

Put several bots in one chat. Mention a bot with **@** to ask it something. Bots can hand work to each other. Press **Cmd+Shift+N** or **Ctrl+Shift+N** for a new group chat.

## Memory

Bots remember facts you share, like your name, your projects or how you like things done. Ask a bot what it remembers, or tell it to forget something.

## Routines

A routine is work a bot does on a schedule or when something happens, like a morning summary or a weekly report. Ask in plain words: "Every Monday at 9, send me a summary of my week." The bot sets it up and posts the result in your chat.

## The bot's computer

Each bot can use its own computer: a browser, files and a terminal. It works there, not on your screen.

- **Take control:** when a site needs your password or a code, open the bot's screen, type it yourself, then hand it back. Gente never types your passwords for you.
- By default the bot's computer runs privately on your own computer.

## Online work

Some jobs work better on a computer in the cloud, like booking a ride or using a site that needs a full browser. This is **online work**. Your plan includes a number of hours of it each month (see [Plans and billing](billing)).

- Gente stops before anything final, like confirming a ride or paying. You confirm it.
- The cloud computer only sees the site the bot is using for that task. Your chats stay on your computer.

## Teach a task

Show a bot how you do something once. It watches the steps and drafts a skill it can repeat later. You review the skill before it is used.

## Approvals

You choose what a bot may do alone and what needs your OK, per app and per action. When a bot needs your OK, a card shows up in the chat. You can keep chatting while it waits.

## Connect apps

Press **Cmd+Shift+M** or **Ctrl+Shift+M** to connect apps like Gmail, Calendar, Slack or GitHub. One connection reaches many apps. A bot only uses an app when a task needs it, and it follows your approval settings.

## Payments

A bot can ask to buy something for you. You approve each purchase yourself in Link. Link gives a one-time card for that purchase. Gente never stores your card.

## Telegram

Talk to your bots from your phone through Telegram. Choose **Connect Telegram…** in the app menu and follow [the steps](troubleshooting#telegram-stopped-answering). Setup takes about a minute.

## Voice and dictation

Talk instead of typing. Gente turns your voice into text on your computer. Your Mac or PC asks once for microphone permission.

## Pages

Bots can build simple pages, like a dashboard or a document, to show their work.
