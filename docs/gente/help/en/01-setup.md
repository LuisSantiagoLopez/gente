# Set up Your AI

Your AI is a private AI helper that runs on your own computer. Setup takes one click and some waiting while it downloads.

## What you need

- A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 26, or a Windows 11 PC (64-bit).
- At least **16 GB of memory**. Your AI's model uses about 11 GB while it works.
- About **20 GB of free disk space**. The model is 8.9 GB, and Your AI keeps room to spare.
- Internet for the first setup. After that, chats work without it.

## Install on a Mac

1. Download Your AI for Mac and open the file.
2. Drag **Your AI** into **Applications**.
3. Open Your AI from Applications.
4. If your Mac says it can't check the app, right-click Your AI, choose **Open**, then **Open** again. You only do this once.

## Install on Windows

1. Download the Your AI installer and open it.
2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. You only do this once.
3. Your AI installs for your user only. It does not need an administrator.

## First run

Click **Get started**. Your AI then does everything by itself:

1. Checks that your computer has enough memory.
2. Installs Ollama, the free app that runs AI models, if you don't have it.
3. Downloads Your AI's model, Swift 1.5 27B (8.9 GB, one time). You see a progress bar. If the internet drops, it picks up where it stopped.
4. Loads the model once to make sure it runs.
5. Starts your private workspace and opens a chat.

Then create your local account. It stays on this computer.

## How long it takes

Most of the time is the 8.9 GB download. On a fast connection that is about 10 minutes. On a slow one it can take an hour. You can leave the window open and do other things.

## Updates

Your AI updates itself. When an update is ready, it installs the next time you quit and open Your AI. You can also choose **Check for Updates** in the app menu.

If you want fixes sooner, open **Help → Your AI Support**, click the gear, and set **Updates** to **Early fixes**. Most people should keep **Regular**.

## Uninstall

- **Mac:** quit Your AI and drag it from Applications to the Trash. Your data stays in your user Library under Application Support/Gente until you delete that folder.
- **Windows:** use **Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Your AI → Uninstall**. Your data stays in your AppData/Roaming/Gente folder until you delete it.

Ollama and the model stay installed. You can remove them from the Ollama app.
